WORKING PRINCIPLE

Mechanical engineering meets embedded control.

Wireless commands direct the robot's movement. The cutting assembly provides grass-cutting capability, while a tank, pump and nozzle form the water sprinkling mechanism. An ultrasonic sensor is included for obstacle detection.

TECHNICAL NOTE

Designed as a prototype.

No claims are made about autonomous operation, cutting performance, working duration, weather resistance, certifications, or warranty until these have been established and verified.

AXION / TECHNOLOGY
BEHIND THE BUILD

Mechanics.
Electronics. Purpose.

Understand the engineering components and operating principle behind AXION's smart gardening prototype.

SYSTEM 01

Movement & chassis

The prototype uses a four-wheel robotic chassis as the foundation for moving its cutting and sprinkling systems around a garden area.

SYSTEM 02

Control & sensing

An Arduino-based controller integrates the robot's electronics. Wireless mobile control and ultrasonic obstacle detection are key capabilities.

SYSTEM 03

Grass cutting

A grass-cutting mechanism is incorporated into the robotic system. Detailed performance and safety specifications are pending verification.

SYSTEM 04

Water sprinkling

The water tank, DC pump and sprinkler nozzle collectively form the watering mechanism of the prototype.
